Core Gameplay Mechanics (Detailed)

Complex Party System

Details

A complex system that both encompasses the layering having multiple parties and multiple party members in each party. Playing each Party at different times during the game.

How it Works

The player can switch between characters in their current party on the fly, allowing them to change tactics quickly and choose the characters that best fit their play-style. As the player progresses through the game they will play various different parties depending on the current state of the game. At the most there will be 4 Seperate parties: Coby (Wiz Kid), Fern, Momo, and The Sisters (Skye, Brooke, Ember). While eventually Fern, Momo, and Coby will be in the same party.

Combat Engine

Details

Complex Melee and Spell Casting combat with unique effects and advantages to each different type of attack. The enemies will also have similar mechanics and complex interactions based on the state of the game. As well as boss fights with stage mechanics.

How it Works

The Player will be able to learn and equip abilities and attacks, having a limited number of equipped abilities at any given time, allowing the player to customize each character's play-style. The enemies will range from simple melee combat, to complex scripted boss fights with multiple mechanics, that sometimes includes using the environment for tactical advantage.

Perk & Progression

Details

The Player will acquire upgrade points via performing certain actions like defeating enemies, completing quests, exploring, crafting, etc. They can spend these points to improve and customize their characters.

How it Works

After obtaining enough upgrade points the player will be to spend their points on either Perks, or improving their abilities. Perks improve the players stats, vitals, or grant new abilities. Each character will be able to learn new abilities from training or Spell Books that can be upgraded X number of times.

World Map Hub

Details

The World Map will act as the Travel Hub for the player. When leaving a zone they will come to a transition that will offer to open the world map for traveling. The World Map will consist of two major continents: The Old World and The New World.

How it Works

The World Map will contain many nodes the player can unlock and travel to. After a node is unlocked it will appear on the map as a new location. Some areas the player will be able to return to, while others will be disabled once the player completes the area. During Travel on the world map, there will be certain areas that can trigger Random Encounters, pulling the player out into a small Encounter Area with additional enemies or NPCs.

Story and Gameplay

Story (Brief)

Finding Fern 2 is about 4 Witch Sisters, their Cat Momo, and a young boy from another realm named Coby. Their journeys will intertwine into a complex story of adventure, mystery, and intrigue.

Gameplay (Brief)

The core gameplay loop consists of the Player progressing the story, completing quests, defeating enemies, dialogue, and upgrading their characters through Perks, Abilities, Equipment, and Crafting.

Narrative Arc (Detailed)

The Game will begin with Coby in the modern world, a normal young boy interested in the fantastical. During the start, Coby’s mother reads him a bedtime story about the world of Finding Fern. The next day Coby wakes up in Fern’s world, confused, where he encounters helpful NPCs.

The story then moves to Fern trying to get Momo back. She discovers a Dark Portal may exist in The New World and runs away again, seeking passage with help from her old friend Eva. Meanwhile, Skye, Brook, and Ember discover Fern is missing and head to the capital city Beacon to charter a boat to the New World.

Fern encounters Coby in the New World. Coby joins her search for Momo. The game then switches to Momo in the Dark World, where he defeats the current Dark Lord and becomes Dark Lord Momo. Back in the New World, the sisters run into trouble with the Inquisition—a group enforcing magical law.

The Dark Cult eventually helps Fern and Coby bring Momo back from the Dark World, believing him to be the new Dark Lord. After reuniting, they are all detained by the Inquisition and forced to join the Academy of Magical Arts. Disliking the Academy, Fern and Coby decide to run away, opening the game up for Finding Fern 3.

Gameplay (Detailed)

The Gameplay focuses heavily on Combat as an Action RPG with character dialogue and questing as a secondary mechanic. The player will defeat enemies, fight bosses, and solve puzzles using their abilities and magic. They will be directed towards the main storyline using questing and dialogue from NPCs and inter-character conversations.

As the player progresses they will unlock new areas they will be able to travel to via the World Map. Certain milestones and chapters the player achieves may lock previous areas of the game. The play will need to spend their Upgrade Points wisely to improve their characters and be able to defeat stronger enemies. The main goal of the game is to complete the core storyline while staying immersed in fast-paced action combat.
